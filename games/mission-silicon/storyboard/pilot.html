Pilot: four decisions before any code

A clickable storyboard of a new first episode, for review before anything is built in Godot. Forkcast has to work on Thursday for two hundred diners on iPhones, Android phones and laptops. Before Aleister writes a line, the player learns the map of a web app and makes the architecture decisions an AI won't flag: where the app runs, what goes on the phone and what stays on the server, whether to build or rent the backend, and how to read the plan the AI hands back.

What changed from version 1: it taught how to phrase a request. This one teaches what to ask for. The player leaves Level One able to name the parts of an app, weigh web against native against cross-platform, and catch a secret key headed for the browser.
